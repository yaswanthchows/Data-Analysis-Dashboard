## Project Insights
Identified the branches with the highest loan disbursement, helping evaluate regional business performance.
Analyzed loan distribution by loan type to understand customer borrowing preferences.
Monitored loan approval rates to assess the efficiency of the loan approval process.
Tracked monthly loan disbursement trends to identify seasonal patterns and business growth.
Compared loan distribution by gender to understand customer demographics.
Evaluated employee-wise loan processing to measure individual productivity and workload.
Analyzed repayment status to identify paid, pending, and defaulted loans, supporting credit risk assessment.
Built interactive KPI cards, charts, maps, and slicers to enable dynamic reporting and faster decision-making.

## Conclusion
The Banking Loan Analysis Dashboard successfully transformed raw banking data into meaningful business insights through interactive visualizations and KPI reporting. By leveraging Microsoft Excel, Power Pivot, and advanced Excel functions, the dashboard enables efficient monitoring of loan performance, branch operations, customer trends, and repayment status, supporting data-driven decision-making.
